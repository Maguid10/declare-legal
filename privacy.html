Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Declare ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we handle and why. We keep it simple, because we keep your data to a minimum.

What we handle

  • Your declaration and goal. Stored on your device. To create your personalized guided visualization, the text of your declaration is sent securely to our backend and to AI providers that generate the script and the voice audio. The resulting audio is cached on our servers so it does not need to be regenerated.
  • Streak and usage. Your progress (streak, completed sessions) is stored locally on your device.
  • Camera. During the ritual the camera is used live as a mirror. Nothing is recorded, saved, or transmitted unless you explicitly choose to.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't ask for your name, address, or unnecessary personal information.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by the App Store, Google Play, and our payments partner. We never see or store your payment details.

Your choices

You can reset your progress at any time in the app's settings. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device.

Children

Declare is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
