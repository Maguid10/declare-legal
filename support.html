How does Declare work?

Every morning and night you look into your own eyes, using your phone as a mirror, and declare your goal as if it is already true. Over time this builds real belief.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Declare. You can cancel anytime.

How do I restore my purchase?

In Declare, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

Where are my video diary recordings stored?

Your video diary stays private on your device. Your recordings are never uploaded.

How do I change my declaration or reminders?

Open Settings inside Declare to edit your declaration and set your morning and evening reminders.
